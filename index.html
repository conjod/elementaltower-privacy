Privacy Policy

Elemental Tower Survivor · com.cjgames.elementaltowersurvivor

Last updated: 8 September 2026

This policy explains what Elemental Tower Survivor collects, why, and how to have it deleted. It covers the Android app and the browser version of the game.

The short version. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, and no ads. It never asks for your name, email address, phone number or location, and it never collects them. What it does send is gameplay data — how far you got, which abilities you bought, how smoothly the game ran — filed under a nickname the game invents for you, not under anything that identifies you.

Who is responsible

The game is made and published by CJ Games, an individual developer. Contact details are at the bottom of this page.

What the game sends, and why

1. Your in-game nickname

The first time you play, the game generates a two-word nickname for you (for example “Ember Warden”) and stores it on your device. You do not type it and are never asked to. You can reroll it for a different random one. This nickname is attached to the data below so that your leaderboard entry and your runs can be told apart from everyone else's.

Because it is invented by the game rather than supplied by you, the nickname does not identify you to us or to anyone else — unless you choose to tell people what yours is.

2. Leaderboard entries

When you set a new personal best, the game submits one entry to the global leaderboard so the board can be shown to all players. The entry contains: your nickname, how long you survived, the wave you reached, how many shop rerolls and revives you used, which tower you played, the date, a label saying which platform you played on (Android, web, and so on), and a short check value used to spot tampered scores.

Only your best run is stored. A new best overwrites the previous one.

3. Run records

At the end of every run — and as a snapshot if you close the game mid-run — the game submits a record of that run. This is used to balance the game and to find performance problems on hardware the developer does not own.

The record contains only gameplay and performance information: your nickname, the build of the game you played, the tower you chose, the wave and time you reached, kills, which abilities you bought and when, damage and healing per ability, which boss relics you took, how the run ended, which towers you have unlocked, how many runs you have finished, and a summary of frame times during the run. It also carries the same platform label as above.

4. Feedback you send

The game has a feedback form. It is entirely optional and nothing is sent unless you write something and tap Send. When you do, the game submits the text you wrote (up to 1,000 characters), your nickname, the date and time, the platform label, your screen resolution, and your device's browser/OS identification string, together with the wave, time and tower of the run you were on. The last three are included so a bug report can be reproduced on the right kind of device.

Please do not put personal information — your real name, email address, or anything else identifying — into the feedback box. If you do, it will be stored along with the rest of your message.

What the game does not collect

  • No account, login, or sign-in of any kind.
  • No real name, email address, phone number, or postal address.
  • No location or GPS data.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone.
  • No advertising identifier. The game contains no advertising SDK and shows no ads.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDK, and no tracking of you across other apps or websites.
  • No payment information. The game is free and has no in-app purchases.

Data stored only on your device

Your save data — unlocked towers, your nickname, settings, tutorial progress and your local best score — is kept in your device's local storage and is not uploaded. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site data in your browser, erases it permanently. There is no cloud save and no way to recover it.

Where the data goes

The leaderboard entries, run records and feedback described above are stored in a Google Firebase Realtime Database hosted in the europe-west1 region (Belgium). Google acts as the data processor and handles the data under Google's Firebase privacy terms. The data is not sold, and is not shared with anyone else.

The leaderboard is public within the game: any player can see the nicknames, times and waves on it. Run records and feedback are not shown to other players.

How long it is kept

Leaderboard entries are kept for as long as the leaderboard exists. Run records and feedback are kept while they are useful for balancing and fixing the game. There is no fixed deletion schedule, and anything you ask to have removed is removed.

Deleting your data

Email the address below and ask, and your leaderboard entry, run records and feedback will be deleted.

Because the game holds nothing that identifies you, the only way to find your data is by your in-game nickname — so please include it. You can see your current nickname on the game's home screen. If you have rerolled it, mention the previous ones you remember.

You can also stop any further data being sent at any time by playing with the device offline, or by uninstalling the game.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect information from them. It shows no advertising and offers no purchases. If you believe a child has sent feedback containing personal information, contact the address below and it will be deleted.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts collecting something new — for example if optional rewarded adverts are added in a future version — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top of this page will change.
